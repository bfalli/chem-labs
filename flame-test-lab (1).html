Flame tests: virtual lab

Heat a few metal compounds in a flame and watch the colors. Record the flame color for each sample on your lab report, identify the unknown, and answer the questions.

How to run a test

  1. Choose a sample below.
  2. Press Dip wire. The wire loop picks up some of the sample.
  3. Press Hold in flame, and watch the flame carefully. The color fades after a few seconds.
  4. Press Clean wire before you test the next sample. A dirty wire will carry leftover color.

Inside an atom: electron jumps

Hold a sample in the flame to see how its electron jumps.

In the flame, the metal ions from the sample become neutral atoms. The diagrams follow one electron in one of those atoms. The Bohr model shows the shells that hold the electrons, but it cannot show s, p, and d subshells, so the jump itself is drawn in the orbital diagram. Energy levels are not drawn to scale.
