Hydrogen peroxide by permanganate titration

Standardize a potassium permanganate solution against sodium oxalate, then use it to find the actual molarity of a drugstore bottle of hydrogen peroxide labeled 3% (about 0.88 M).

Buret close-up

Read the bottom of the meniscus at eye level. Estimate one digit past the smallest marking, to ±0.01 mL.

Procedure
  1. Weigh a sodium oxalate sample into the flask and record its mass.
  2. Dissolve it in water, acidify with sulfuric acid, and heat to about 80 °C.
  3. Fill the buret and record the initial reading.
  4. Add KMnO₄ until a faint pink color lasts 30 seconds. Go slowly near the end.
  5. Record the final reading. Run at least two trials.
  1. Make the dilution once: 10.00 mL of drugstore H₂O₂ diluted to 250.00 mL.
  2. Pipet a 25.00 mL portion of the diluted solution into the flask and acidify it.
  3. Record the initial buret reading.
  4. Titrate to a faint pink that lasts 30 seconds, then record the final reading.
  5. Run at least two trials.
