How long will that marble statue last?

Marble is mostly calcium carbonate, and acid rain slowly dissolves it. Measure how fast CaCO₃ reacts with hydrochloric acid by tracking the mass of CO₂ that escapes, then find the rate law and test how surface area and temperature change the rate.

Procedure
  1. Choose the conditions for this trial.
  2. Pour 25.0 mL of HCl into a clean flask on the balance. The balance reading includes the flask’s loose cotton plug.
  3. Weigh about 2 g of CaCO₃ and record the exact mass.
  4. Lift the cotton plug, add the CaCO₃, put the plug back loosely (it lets CO₂ out but keeps acid spray in), and start the timer.
  5. The balance reading is recorded every 10 s for the first 2 minutes, then every 30 s, up to 10 minutes.
  6. Copy the data table into your handout, then clean up and run the next trial.
